\section{Vim Survival Guide} 

Sooner or later you will encounter Vim. 

Open a file: 

\begin{lstlisting} 
vim file.txt 
\end{lstlisting} 

\subsection{Insert Mode} 

Press 

\begin{lstlisting} 
i 
\end{lstlisting} 

to begin editing. 

\subsection{Return to Command Mode} 

Press 

\begin{lstlisting} 
Esc 
\end{lstlisting} 

\subsection{Common Commands} 

Save: 

\begin{lstlisting} 
:w 
\end{lstlisting} 

Quit: 

\begin{lstlisting} 
:q 
\end{lstlisting} 

Save and quit: 

\begin{lstlisting} 
:wq 
\end{lstlisting} 

Quit without saving: 

\begin{lstlisting} 
:q! 
\end{lstlisting} 

\subsection{Most Important Command} 

If you become stuck: 

\begin{lstlisting} 
Esc :q! 
\end{lstlisting}
