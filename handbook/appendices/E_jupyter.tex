\section{Jupyter Notebooks} 

Jupyter allows interactive computing through a web browser. 

\subsection{Start Jupyter} 

\begin{lstlisting} 
jupyter lab --no-browser 
\end{lstlisting} 

The server will display a URL and port number. 

\subsection{Create an SSH Tunnel} 

From your laptop: 

\begin{lstlisting} 
ssh -L 8888:localhost:8888 username@pelee 
\end{lstlisting} 

\subsection{Open in a Browser} 

Open 

\begin{lstlisting} 
http://localhost:8888 
\end{lstlisting} 

in your browser.
