\section{Monitoring Resources}

Before running large jobs, monitor resource usage.

\subsection{CPU and Memory}

\begin{lstlisting}
htop
\end{lstlisting}

or

\begin{lstlisting}
top
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Disk Usage}

Available space:

\begin{lstlisting}
df -h
\end{lstlisting}

Directory sizes:

\begin{lstlisting}
du -sh *
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Exercise}

Determine how much free disk space is available and identify the largest directory in your project.
